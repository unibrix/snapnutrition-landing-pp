MyFitnessPal Alternative for iPhone

An honest comparison of SnapNutrition AI, MyFitnessPal, Cronometer, Lose It!, and Cal AI — including where each one wins.

Last updated: July 3, 2026 · Prices verified July 2026

Why people look for MyFitnessPal alternatives

MyFitnessPal is still the biggest name in calorie tracking, and its food database is genuinely unmatched. The complaints that send people looking elsewhere are consistent, though:

  • Ads on the free tier
  • Features moving behind Premium — most famously the barcode scanner in 2022, and today the AI features (meal scan, voice log)
  • Price — Premium is $19.99/month or $79.99/year, Premium+ is $24.99/month or $99.99/year (July 2026)
  • Mandatory account and the data profile that comes with it
  • Heavier interface than a simple photo-first logger

Where SnapNutrition AI fits

SnapNutrition AI takes a different approach from every app in the table: instead of a subscription that covers AI costs plus margin, it uses BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) — you plug in your own OpenAI or Anthropic API key and pay the provider directly, typically $0.01–0.03 per scan. That means:

  • No mandatory subscription — AI photo, voice, and text logging are free with your key
  • No ads, no account — nothing to sign up for, no profile to build
  • Privacy by architecture — in BYOK mode your photos go from your device straight to the AI provider; there is no app backend
  • Apple Watch app and widgets — log by voice from your wrist, see progress on your Lock Screen
  • Optional Auto Mode ($2.99/month or $29.99/year, 3-day trial) if you'd rather skip API-key setup — still cheaper than every premium tier in the table

Honest tradeoffs — when you should NOT switch

A comparison page you can trust has to include this part:

  • Stay with MyFitnessPal if you rely on its huge food database, log exercise in the same app, use the community features, or need Android support. SnapNutrition AI is iOS-only.
  • Pick Cronometer if micronutrients are the point — tracking iron, B12, or zinc with verified data is its home turf, and AI photo estimates can't compete on that.
  • Pick Lose It! if you want a conventional, coach-like weight-loss app and are fine paying ~$40/year.
  • Pick Cal AI if you want AI photo scanning with zero setup and don't mind a subscription.
  • Skip SnapNutrition AI's BYOK mode if creating an API key sounds like a chore — though Auto Mode exists exactly for that, and it's still the cheapest subscription in the table.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free MyFitnessPal alternative for iPhone?

It depends on what you want. For AI photo scanning without a subscription, SnapNutrition AI is free with your own API key (BYOK), with no ads or account. For the deepest micronutrient data, Cronometer's free tier is strong. For the cheapest conventional premium, Lose It! is about $39.99/year.

Why do people switch away from MyFitnessPal?

The most common reasons: ads on the free tier, the barcode scanner and AI features moving behind Premium, repeated price increases, mandatory account creation, and a heavier interface than simple photo-based loggers.

Is SnapNutrition AI really free if it uses AI?

Yes — through the BYOK model. You create your own OpenAI or Anthropic API key and pay the provider directly, typically $0.01–0.03 per scan (around $1–3/month for three meals a day). The app charges nothing. An optional Auto Mode subscription ($2.99/month or $29.99/year) exists for people who prefer to skip the setup.

What does MyFitnessPal do better?

A much larger crowd-sourced food database (20M+ foods), exercise tracking, recipes, community features, and an Android app. SnapNutrition AI is iOS-only and focused on fast AI-based food logging.
